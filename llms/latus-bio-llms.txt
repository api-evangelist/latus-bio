# Latus Bio

> Latus Bio is a clinical-stage gene therapy company engineering novel adeno-associated virus (AAV) capsids to deliver genetic medicines to precise cell types in the central nervous system and peripheral tissues. The company screens tens of millions of novel capsid variants to build a proprietary database of tissue-targeting "delivery zip codes," pairing engineered capsids with optimized routes of administration. Lead programs are LTS-201 for Huntington's disease (AAV.DB-3 capsid) and LTS-101 for CLN2 disease across CNS and ocular delivery (AAV.Ep+ capsid). Offices and laboratories are in Philadelphia, PA and Boston's Seaport district. Investors include DCVC Bio, 8VC, BioAdvance, Samsung Venture Investment, and KDB.
